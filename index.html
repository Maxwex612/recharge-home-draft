/01 · Verifies

MINE

Compliance and passport

Proved at the source, not reconstructed later.

MINE verifies where each batch came from, how much there is and what it is, and records it in the passport. Every entry is signed, time-stamped and anchored to a blockchain, so any later change is visible. An Adaptive AI engine, software that adjusts as new data arrives, reads the passport record across the network, not one market at a time.

The same passport record serves the regulator, the buyer and the financier, and makes the tonne tokenizable. MINE never holds funds or assets: when an agreed condition is met, it issues a signed instruction and a separately licensed party, such as a bank, acts on it.

/03 · Moves

RAW

Brokerage and movement

The route to market for a verified tonne.

RAW prices, routes, trades and settles verified material against the passport record, to the best-paying permitted outlet once freight, storage and treatment are counted. RAW also handles the shipment documentation and duty of care that cross-border moves require.

RAW works three ways: back-to-back buying and selling of the same shipment, brokerage for a fee, and gate fees for accepting material and routing it to the right permitted treatment. RAW earns on the movement, not on the metal. It can move what MINE has verified, what Fortress holds and what Regenerate recovers.

Delivery due 2027

Why now

Mandatory in Europe
from 18 February 2027.

From that date, covered batteries cannot be placed on the EU market without a digital battery passport. The duty falls on whoever places them there, yet most of the data sits upstream, out of their reach.

The law

The EU battery passport

A digital record for every covered battery, opened with a QR code on the battery. It states what the battery is made of, how much of it is recycled and its carbon footprint. Whoever places the battery on the EU market answers for it, and cannot hand that duty to a supplier.

Our record

The Recharge passport

It starts at the mine or the scrap pile, long before a battery exists, and every step is scanned in as it happens. By the time the battery is built, the data the EU passport asks for is already on the record, ready to feed it. The same record proves what went on the market and what came back to be recycled.

FROM 2025

Carbon footprint declarations phase in as the Commission adopts its methodology.

18 FEB 2027

The digital battery passport becomes mandatory for electric vehicle, industrial, home storage and light transport batteries.

END 2027

Recycling must recover 50% of lithium, and 63% of portable batteries must be collected.

2031

From August, minimum recycled content applies: 85% lead, 16% cobalt, and 6% each of lithium and nickel. By year end, 80% of lithium must be recovered.
